Hello and welcome to my website!

I'm a 4th year undergraduate student at the University of Toronto in the Astronomy & Physics Specialist and Mathematics Major programs.

My research interests are nearly anything to do with computational astrophysics, though magnetohydrodynamics and generallu compact objects are the biggest right now. I am also intrigued by astroparticle physics and hope to learn more about it and dark matter.

I'm also interested in the nuclear energy sector, both fission and fusion. I hope the future will see much more nuclear energy and I think it will be a big part of our switch to a emmission-free planet.
